## Text Tutorial
#### Go to the lecture you want to download.
#### Open Devtools by clicking ctrl+shift+i.
#### Or click on the 3 dots on the top right hand corner then go to more tools then dev tools.
#### Open the Eme logger extension.
#### Change the extension from hex to base64.
#### Reload the page (only once).
#### Now click on download log in EME logger.
#### Open the .txt in notepad and click ctrl + a then ctrl + c to copy it.
#### Extract the pw-downloader.zip from releases
#### Open keypair.exe and paste the copied logs. Then click ctrl + z and enter
#### Ensure keypair.txt is generated in the same folder
#### Now go back to devtools in the tab and go to the network tab.
#### Search master and right click any one of the search results go to copy and copy as url (dont click copy as cUrl)
#### Now open downloader.exe and paste the link
#### Choose your video quality and wait till the window closes itself
#### Your file has been downloaded
## IMPORTANT NOTES
### This only gives you access to download lectures you can already view.
### Doesnt work on lectures uploaded to youtube as well. Use a simple youtube downloader for that
### BE SURE TO CLICK CLEAR LOG ON EME EXTENSION BEFORE EVERY REFRESH
### CHANGE EME LOGS FROM HEX TO BASE64
